Before you start

Akwaaba shares your name, car, registration plate and WhatsApp number with each traveller you are assigned, so they can find and contact you. You see your passenger's name, phone number and drop-off address. Passenger details disappear from Akwaaba 90 days after the trip. Your account and trip history are kept while you drive with us and for 2 years after. Read the full privacy policy

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing astrovenzav@gmail.com. If you withdraw consent to vetting checks, we can no longer send you pickups.

Fares: passengers pay you directly when you meet them, in cash or by mobile money to your own number. You keep the whole fare. Akwaaba only charges the GHS 100 monthly subscription.
